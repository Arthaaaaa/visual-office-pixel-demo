A little pixel office where the coworkers keep moving, the tasks keep ticking, and somebody is probably on a coffee break.

## 🪴 Take a peek inside

- **A living office** with six rooms to explore.
- **Busy little agents** who wander between their desks, meetings, and breaks.
- **A task board** for queued, active, and completed work.
- **Office overview** with productivity numbers and a live activity feed.
- **Day and night moods** that change the look of the office.
- **Lo-fi ambience and sound effects** for a softer desk-side atmosphere.

## 🎮 Your tiny desk crew

Pan around the office, zoom in on a room, check on an agent, or send someone to grab a warm drink. The dashboard keeps an eye on tasks while the office carries on in the background.

## 🌙 A small note

The office is a cozy little simulation. Its agents, tasks, activity, and productivity figures are part of the demo, not a live connection to a real team.
